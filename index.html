Platform features

Everything you need for smarter healthcare decisions

💊
Complete drug database
Search any medicine by brand name or generic. Get full indications, dosage, side effects, contraindications, and drug interactions — all reviewed for the Bangladeshi market.
🤖
AI symptom checker
Describe your symptoms in plain Bangla or English. Our AI model analyzes patterns and suggests possible conditions, urgency level, and primary care steps — with clear medical disclaimers.
📍
Nearest clinic & lab finder
After your symptom check, instantly see the nearest verified clinics, hospitals, and diagnostic labs on a map. Filter by specialty, fees, and availability.
👨‍⚕️
Verified doctor directory
Find BMDC-registered doctors by specialty, area, consultation fees, and patient reviews. Every profile is verified. Book appointments directly through the platform.
🐄
Veterinary medicine segment
A dedicated section for veterinary drugs — covering livestock, poultry, and pets. Essential for Bangladesh's agricultural economy, with dosage guides for common animals.
🇧🇩
Bangla-first design
Full Bangla language support across the entire platform. No confusing medical jargon — all information is written in clear, everyday language for Bangladeshi users.

How it works

Three steps to smarter health decisions

1
Enter your symptoms or search a drug
Type your symptoms in Bangla or English — like "মাথাব্যথা, জ্বর, গা ব্যথা"/ "headache, fever, body pain" or search a medicine/generic name. curabd.io understands both.
2
Get AI-powered analysis & information
Our AI model analyzes your input and returns possible conditions ranked by likelihood, primary care steps, urgency level, and complete drug information if you searched a medicine.
3
Find care near you — instantly
Based on your result, see the nearest verified clinics, labs, and doctors on a map. Filter by specialty and fee range. Book an appointment or lab test directly.
